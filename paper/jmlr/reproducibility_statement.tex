\section*{Reproducibility Statement}

The public research repository at
\url{https://github.com/Null-Square/memory-frontier}
contains the implementation, exact theorem fixtures, adversarial counterexamples,
paper-figure generator, and release automation. The complete regression suite is
run in continuous integration. Headline exact regressions cover the
support/operator/loss construction-order hierarchy, dormant forward equivalence,
regular reparameterization behavior, affine and softmax-boundary dynamics, the
independent linear state-space validation, and near-degeneracy counterexamples.

Two broader evidence programs remain outside CI by design: the frozen
1,000-controller census within one exact forward-equivalence class and the
illustrative linear-state-space optimization audit. Their seeds, configurations,
and expected headline outputs are frozen in the repository. The four main figures
are produced by a deterministic script and are covered by a headless render smoke
test. A reviewer-facing reproduction guide maps each paper claim to its exact test
or experiment and gives a short path for installation, testing, evidence
reproduction, figure generation, and paper compilation.
